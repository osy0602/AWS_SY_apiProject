# 🖥 Analysis of Overseas Direct Purchase Trends and Their Impact on the Korean Commerce Market

### 📖 1. Overview
This project provides a data-driven analysis of how the recent surge in overseas direct purchases impacts the **Korean online shopping market**. The goal is to offer meaningful insights for prospective entrepreneurs and current business owners in Korea. By analyzing the correlation between overseas direct purchase trends and Korean e-commerce closure rates over the past five years, we aim to provide an objective understanding of ongoing market shifts.

### 🎯 2. Background
In recent years, the commerce market has undergone significant changes:

**Expansion of the Global E-commerce Market**: Led by massive sales events like China's 11.11 Singles' Day, the global e-commerce market has experienced explosive growth.

**Increase in Overseas Direct Purchases**: As cross-border shopping becomes more accessible, the direct purchasing of overseas goods by domestic consumers has become commonplace.

**Intensified Competition Among Domestic Malls**: Fierce market competition has led to high business closure rates, emerging as a major challenge for the domestic industry.

**The Importance of Data-Driven Decisions**: In this complex market environment, the need for accurate, data-driven analysis is more critical than ever for successful business launches and sustainability.

### 🚀 3. Key Features

#### 1. Data Trend Visualization

- We collect and visualize data on the volume of overseas direct purchases and domestic online shopping mall closures over the past five years.

#### 2. Correlation Analysis by Business Sector

- By comparing growth and decline rates across the dataset, the project identifies which domestic e-commerce sectors show the highest correlation with the rise in overseas direct purchases. This allows users to intuitively understand which sectors are most sensitive to cross-border shopping trends.
